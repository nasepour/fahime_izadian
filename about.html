About

TECHNIQUE, STYLE AND VISION

Making space for observation.

In the Iranians dwells the art of valor,They bow to none- not even the raging rhinoceros. One in heart, and knowing their Divine Creator,They ask no praise from the stars for their goodness

Fahime Izadian was born in 1980.05.31 in bajak district, Qom her family discovered her talent in painting when she was 3, she was pupil of maestro Narges Ahanin Panjeh as a child and she was only inspired by her , she was smitten with manners and methods OF Dr HosseinTabakhian , who was a graduate of ENGLISH Translation from United states, and became his pupil until she became an adult, she has Bachelor of Arts in ENGLISH LITERATURE from KHORAM ABAD state university ( 2011) and Mater of ARTS in Translation Techniques from TEHRAN SCIENCE and RESEARCH UNIVERSITY (2014) she was hired by National Drilling Company in 2015 as a Translation Senior Expert for INTERNATIONAL contracts in KISH ISLAND , Mastery over ENGLISH, French, Spanish , Arabic didn't satisfy her, therefore , after years and years , she found absolute power in Silence (Painting) . she started the project of oil painting of Glance of Prophets (Negah-E-Peygambaran) in 2021. Portraits and notes selected by the artist are inspired by her feelings, since the mountains and the worlds are not able to draw the grandeur of glance of prophets, she is creating works of prophets with the hope that the foregoing might lead to emergence of more works of arts in the universe.
